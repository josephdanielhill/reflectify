Reflect on your job. Visualize the result.

Turn a few honest answers into a report worth keeping.

Reflectify has two steps: get a prompt to interview yourself with any AI, then upload the JSON it gives you back here for a visual report you can download. Nothing you write is ever sent anywhere — it all stays in this browser tab.

💬

1. Get your prompt

Copy a ready-made prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI you like. It will ask how many months you're reflecting on, your job title, then guide you through tailored questions about achievements, challenges, regrets, skill growth, and goals.

📊

2. Upload your answers

When your AI finishes the interview, it will give you a JSON file (or a JSON block you can save as one). Upload it here to generate a visual report you can download as a PDF or an image.
