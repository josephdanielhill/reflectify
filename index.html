Why I built this

Reflection is a gift. I built tools to make it easy to give it to yourself.

I built Reflectify out of my own practice. Life moves fast, and I found that without a regular check-in, the weeks blur together and the important moments quietly slip by. So I created a way to slow down and really listen to myself, one thoughtful question at a time.

These tools helped me see my own patterns more clearly, celebrate what was going well, and gently face what needed attention. The more I used them, the more I wanted to share them. I hope they help you go a little deeper into how you are feeling and doing, and shine a light on the parts of your life that matter most.
